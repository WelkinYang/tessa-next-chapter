06 / From research to applications

A practical next move.

The goal is a smaller set of roles you can explain convincingly. Use the company research to find the right team, then judge the vacancy on its actual requirements.

01

Build two focused résumés.

Transformation / portfolio version: lead with American Airlines and Southwest—scope, decision-making, resources, budgets, executive governance, and delivery. Implementation / operations version: lead with Crunchyroll—launches, repeatable processes, global coordination, and escalation reduction. Keep the employment history consistent; change the emphasis.

02

Turn scope into evidence.

Prepare three short examples: a capacity tradeoff you resolved; a budget or forecast discrepancy you clarified; and a cross-team change that improved delivery. Explain the starting problem, what you personally owned, how you made the decision, and how the outcome was measured. Separate your contribution from the whole team's result.

03

Screen the role before investing heavily.

Read responsibilities before the title. Check daily language, required technical depth, direct-report expectations, location, and employing entity. Clarify foreign-national eligibility and any employer work-authorization support with the recruiter. If Mandarin is essential and you cannot work in Mandarin, treat that as a material gap rather than assuming English will substitute.

04

Make the outreach specific.

Reference one actual responsibility from the job description and one relevant result from your history. Ask whether the role is focused on transformation governance, hands-on engineering, commercial account ownership, or operational execution. These are different jobs even when all use “program manager.”

The strongest evidence to carry forward

  • Transformation scope: three portfolios, 64+ products, and initiatives affecting 1,582+ people.
  • Resource planning: up to 15 concurrent products and 30+ transformation coaches.
  • Governance: nine architecture teams, contracts, forecasting, and senior-stakeholder reporting.
  • Execution: 10+ global launches, 11+ improvement initiatives, and reported 43% fewer late escalations and blockers.

Keep the claims precise

  • Explain the approximately $3M variance issue as documented in your profile; do not automatically call it realized cash savings.
  • Cloud architecture education supports technical fluency; establish hands-on architecture or engineering depth separately where required.
  • Describe military experience through coordination, readiness, logistics, and leadership relevant to the civilian role.
  • Use the actual credential issuer and title. The supplied PMP-labelled entry is not verified as a PMI-issued certification.
Questions worth asking the recruiter +
  1. What business change or delivery problem should this role solve in its first six months?
  2. Is the work primarily portfolio governance, implementation, product ownership, or hands-on technical delivery?
  3. Which languages are used in daily meetings, written documentation, and stakeholder communication?
  4. Which team and legal entity employ the role, and is the position actually based in mainland China?
  5. Can the team consider an overseas applicant, and what employer support is available for required work authorization?
  6. How are success, decision-making authority, staffing responsibility, and budget ownership defined?
