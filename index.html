04 / Make the next move

A few useful next steps.

Choose two versions of your story.

Create one résumé for IT program / implementation delivery and another for global operations / content launches. Lead with results, scale, and executive communication.

Ask the three screening questions early.

Is this role physically based in mainland China? Can all essential responsibilities be performed in English? Does this team support the necessary work authorization for a US applicant relocating from abroad?

Make every claim easy to verify.

Keep the $3M Southwest figure tied to forecasting variance discrepancies, not newly generated revenue. Check the issuer of the listed “PMP” credential before describing it as PMI’s PMP; the supplied profile lists a different training organization. Do not infer proficiency from residence abroad.

Define what a good offer means to you.

Choose acceptable cities, compensation, travel, working hours, relocation timing, and support needs. Ask about the actual team’s time zones and decision-making before prioritizing a brand name.

Bring your leadership story forward.

Describe your military and overseas work through operations planning, stakeholder coordination, readiness, and high-pressure execution. Use only experience and details you are authorized to share.
